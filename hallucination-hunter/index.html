Can you spot AI’s mistakes?

Sometimes AI gives a wrong answer that sounds correct. This is called a hallucination. AI does not lie on purpose. It writes what sounds likely, and sometimes that is wrong.

Here are 8 AI answers. Some are true. Some are false. All of them sound equally sure.

What you just learned

  1. AI sounds just as sure when it is wrong as when it is right. A confident answer can still be wrong.
  2. Watch for the usual traps: wrong numbers and units, mixed-up facts about famous people, and sources that do not exist.
  3. Don’t just trust AI. Check important facts in a reliable source: an official website, a textbook, or the original paper.
