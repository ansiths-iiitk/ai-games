Can you guess what AI will say next?

A chatbot like ChatGPT writes by predicting the next token (a word or part of a word) from everything written so far. Then it repeats that step to build the whole answer.

In this game we use whole words to keep it simple.

Pick the word you think AI would choose. Guess AI’s favourite word. Get 10 points!

What you just learned

  1. A chatbot predicts the next token and repeats that step to write an answer.
  2. The words before it change the prediction. “Python” means a language in one sentence and a snake in the next.
  3. AI can predict a word without knowing if it’s true. Always check.
