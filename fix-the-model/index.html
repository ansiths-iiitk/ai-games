Look at the red ones. Is the AI looking at colour or size?

The AI is looking at size, not colour. In the training data, every ripe mango was big and every unripe mango was small. So “big” became a shortcut for “ripe”. Small ripe mangoes and big unripe mangoes fool it. This is bias in the training data, the same thing you saw in the live demo.

What you just learned

  1. AI learns whatever pattern it finds in the examples, even one we did not want, like size or background.
  2. Good examples help AI learn better. You did not change the AI at all. You only changed the examples, and the score changed.
  3. Show AI different kinds of examples: small and big, green and yellow, in every combination.
